# Data Platform and Ingestion

Movement in the warehouse-and-transformation layer: Snowflake's semantic and governance features (Advanced Semantics, Semantic Studio, Horizon Context), dbt Core's 2.0 progression and dbt Projects on Snowflake's CI capabilities, and the ingestion-layer shift toward agent-written pipelines (dlt) that pushes the real bottleneck upstack to modelling.
